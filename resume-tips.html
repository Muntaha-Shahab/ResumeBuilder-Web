Resume Writing Tips

Follow these proven tips to create an ATS-friendly and professional resume.

📄 Keep resume to 1 page
⚡ Use strong action verbs
🎨 Avoid unnecessary graphics
🎯 Tailor resume to the job
✂ Maintain relevance and brevity
🧠 Highlight key skills strategically
✅ Ensure accuracy & professional tone
🤖 Include ATS-friendly keywords
📞 Use updated contact information
